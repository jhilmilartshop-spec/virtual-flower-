# jhilmiil — virtual flower house

a garden of daisies everywhere. lilies at dusk. an orchid arch against the last light.

build a bouquet from a curated collection of blooms — pick your flowers, choose how 
they're gathered, add a ribbon and wrap, write a note. then share it as a link, 
scan a qr code, or send it by email. something handpicked, wrapped with care.

## flowers available

daisies, lilies, chrysanthemums, orchids, wildflowers, tulips, roses, peonies, 
ranunculus, sunflowers, baby's breath.

## how it's gathered

- hand-tied round — the classic symmetric dome
- cascading — trailing bridal-style, stems flowing downward  
- wild gather — loose, asymmetric, meadow-picked
- tiered formal — structured rows
